# Saathi — pitch video demo script

Record this as one take: you on camera, then the product.
Tone: Uber 2008, not a TED talk. Short sentences. No “AI therapist.” No “disrupting grief.”
This is a **working MVP**. Say that. Judges respect honesty more than a fake Series A.

Total runtime: **~2:10**. Elevator-only cut: stop at 0:55.

---

## How this is built (so you perform it right)

Uber did not open with “we built a logistics marketplace.”
They opened with a pain everyone already had: *cabs in 2008 suck.*

Airbnb: hotels are expensive and sold out.
YC Demo Day: one sentence a tired person understands, then proof.

Your Uber-line is not “we built a Tavus PAL.”
It is: **grief in India has nowhere to go at 1am.**

Status quo (the “call a cab”):
- Family WhatsApp
- “Be strong”
- A counsellor at ₹2,000–₹4,000 with a waitlist

Your tap-a-button:
- One named person on a video call. No login. Tonight.

Proof, because you have no revenue: **the product is live.** Then Pulse. Then the limits of the MVP, said out loud.

## 15-second cold open (hook — do not skip)

**ON CAMERA.** Dark room, phone face-down, one lamp. You look at the lens. Do not smile yet.

> WhatsApp is asleep. A counsellor has a queue.

## Full video script (~2:10)

### 0:00–0:12  HOOK
**ON CAMERA.** Same energy as the cold open. Slightly slower.

> It's 1am in this country.
> Someone you love is gone — or a marriage, or a shop, or an exam.
> You cannot wake the house.

### 0:12–0:28  PROBLEM (make it hurt, like Uber’s “cabs suck”)
> Grief in India is private, late, and unsayable.
> Friends stop asking. Relatives say be strong.
> Paid therapy is weeks away and still stigmatised.
> 1am has no waiting room.

### 0:28–0:40  WHY THE ALTERNATIVES FAIL
> WhatsApp is not a session. It is an audience.
> A helpline is a stranger on a number.
> An app with a chatbot is a text box at the worst moment of your life.
> People do not need another form. They need a face.

### 0:40–0:55  SOLUTION (one sentence — this is the elevator)
**ON CAMERA, then smash-cut to the landing.**

> Saathi is a video call with Maya.
> One AI companion, for India, when there is nobody left to talk to.
> No sign-up. Nothing you say is stored. She is not a counsellor — and we say that before you join.

**This is the line if they only remember one thing:**
> Someone to talk to at 1am, without waking the house.

### 0:55–1:40  DEMO (show, don’t narrate features)
**SCREEN RECORD at 127.0.0.1:5173 or the Render URL.**
You talk over it. Mouse slow. No scrolling around.

| Time | On screen | You say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:55 | Landing: bear, **Saathi**, looping video, Join | This is the product. One person. Not a face picker. |
| 1:02 | Tap **Teen** or **Adult 30–45** | Optional. Maya adapts for a student, a sandwich-generation adult, or a senior. |
| 1:08 | **Join a video call with Maya** → disclosure | First: she is AI. Crisis lines on screen. Then the room. |

| 1:38 | **End call** | |

If Tavus is busy, do **not** fake it. Cut to fallback chat and say:
> Video is one live room on this MVP. If it is taken, you can still talk. We would rather show you the truth.

### 1:40–1:55  WHY NOW
> India has the world’s highest youth suicide rate in the 15–29 band. Student deaths are up. Mid-life is a sandwich of kids and ageing parents. Elders are living more alone.
> Video AI finally looks like a person, not a puppet. That is the wedge. Not a 40-page workbook.

### 1:55–2:05  TRACTION / MVP (honest, intern-judge proof)
**SCREEN: Pulse.**

> This is a working MVP. North star is not downloads. It is **calls started**.
> Pulse counts landing, unique visitors, and live rooms. It never stores speech.
> Limits we will tell you ourselves: one concurrent room, about five minutes, Render can be cold. That is the stack. The idea is bigger than the plan.

### 2:05–2:10  CLOSE
**ON CAMERA.** Small nod. No “thank you for your time” speech.

> Saathi. With you.
> The ask is not a miracle. It is: does this feel useful — or does it feel wrong?
> If it feels useful, here is the link.

**End card (3 seconds, no voice):**
Saathi · with you
`https://<render-url>`

## What not to say (this kills the room)

- “We are an AI therapist / we diagnose / we replace counselling.”
- “Uber for grief.” (Cute. Wrong. Grief is not a ride.)
- Feature soup: Tavus, Raven, Sparrow, PAL ID.
- A suicide statistic as the first line. Hook with the night. Put the stat in *why now*.
- Apologising for the MVP. State the limits once, then move.

## How to shoot

1. Phone on a stack of books. Eye-level. Quiet room.
2. Record the **hook + elevator** first, to camera.
3. Then a clean screen recording of Join → name → breath → End → Pulse.
4. Cut in iMovie / CapCut: you (0:00–0:55) · product (0:55–1:40) · Pulse (1:40–1:55) · you (close).
5. If Maya dies mid-take, keep the take and say the room closed. That is more intern-credible than a second fake.

**Music:** none, or a single low drone under the hook. Never a stock “inspiring startup” track on a grief product.
